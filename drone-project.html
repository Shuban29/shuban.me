Airframe 3D CAD Architecture

High-performance multi-rotor quadcopter platform designed with an aerospace-grade hybrid carbon fiber and lightweight aluminum skeleton. Optimized for maximum torsional rigidity, high thrust-to-weight ratio, and clean component packaging.

Click to Zoom
Fully Rendered SolidWorks Airframe Full Assembly

Isometric render showcasing the quad-arm truss layout, central battery enclosure with custom Shuban debossing, ESC modules, four high-RPM brushless DC outrunner motors, and red polymer propellers.

Key Subsystems & Functions

  • Truss Arms & Chassis: Four lightweight CNC Aluminum 7075-T6 truss arms bolted to a Thornel VCB-20 carbon cloth base plate, resisting bending moments generated during high-speed maneuvers.
  • Propulsion System: Four high-RPM brushless motors paired with high-efficiency red nylon polymer propellers to deliver responsive collective lift and aggressive yaw authority.
  • Integrated Electronics Stack: Internal PCB distribution board fabricated from E-Glass fiber, routing high-amperage power between the centralized LiPo battery, ESCs, and flight controller.
  • Vibration Isolation: POM Acetal copolymer spacers and brass standoffs dampening motor harmonic frequencies to protect sensitive onboard IMU gyro sensors.

Final Vehicle Architecture: Concept 3

Designed to conquer extreme ramp gradients up to 50° under tight kit and single-motor electrical constraints. Concept 3 traded top speed for extreme mechanical advantage, 4-wheel drive contact patch distribution, and rigid chassis anti-flex bracing.

Click to Zoom
Concept 3 3D CAD Prototype Race Winner

Render showing the longitudinal central driveshaft, front and rear 24:1 worm gear reductions, lowered battery box, and structural longitudinal cross-bracing.

Engineering Specifications

  • Drive Layout: All-Wheel Drive (AWD) distributing tractive effort across four tire contact patches to prevent single-axle wheel slippage on steep angles.
  • Gearing Strategy: 24:1 worm gear reduction maximizing torque multiplication and acting as an inherent mechanical anti-rollback brake on steep inclines.
  • Packaging & CG: Flat central battery placement and low-slung ballast below the motor to suppress front-axle pitch-back rotation on 40°–50° ramps.
  • Structural Anti-Flex: Locked gear center-to-center geometry with boxed cross-bracing to prevent high torque separation under extreme ramp resistance.
  • Vehicle Dimensions & Mass: 24.5 × 13.8 × 7.3 cm • 252 g dry mass / 437 g race trim mass.

Ramp Free-Body Vector Diagram Physics Model

Vector resolution of tractive force, normal reaction force, and down-slope gravitational component as ramp angle θ increases from 0° to 50°.

Core Governing Equations

1. Downslope Gravity Force:
F_parallel = m • g • sin(θ)
At 50°, sin(50°) = 0.766; over 76% of vehicle weight acts as direct resisting force against wheel rotation.
2. Friction & Traction Limit:
F_traction = μ • F_normal = μ • m • g • cos(θ)
Normal force drops by 35% on 50° ramp (cos 50° = 0.643), demanding 4-wheel drive to utilize all available tire grip.
3. Motor Power Constraint:
P = τ • ω
With finite battery/motor electrical wattage, multiplying torque τ fundamentally requires lower wheel angular speed ω.

Data-Driven Concept Evolution

Systematic engineering progression testing 3 distinct drivetrain layouts against ramp angle thresholds, converting empirical failure points into architectural upgrades.

Concept Selection Decision Matrix Iterative Prototyping

Direct comparison between high-speed RWD 1:5, stability-biased FWD 1.4:1 with differential, and the ultimate AWD 24:1 worm drive configuration.

Drivetrain Performance Comparison

Concept Layout & Ratio Design Philosophy Test Evidence & Outcome Decision & Next Step
Concept 1 RWD 1:5 (Gear-on-Gear) Pure speed on flat ground with low center of gravity Fast on flat (1.745s average), but motor completely stalled at 10° ramp Abandoned speed bias; recognized wheel torque as true limiting bottleneck
Concept 2 FWD 1.4:1 (Differential) Front-wheel traction & central battery balance Climbed 10° (4.27s), 20° (4.06s), 30° (4.36s), but stalled at 40° Proved traction was improved, but single-axle torque was still insufficient
Concept 3 (Race) AWD 24:1 (Worm Reduction) Maximum torque multiplication & anti-rollback stability Completed all runs from 0° to 50°; 100% reliability under extreme load Selected for official race; validated torque-for-speed trade-off
